The cost to build a mobile app in Kenya in 2026 ranges from about KES 50,000 to 150,000 for a simple app, KES 150,000 to 500,000 for an app with accounts and M-Pesa, and KES 500,000 to 2 million or more for a complex platform. Store fees and yearly maintenance come on top.

However, the right number for you depends on a few decisions you can control. This guide explains them, so you can budget with confidence and compare quotes fairly.

- **Simple app:** 3 to 5 screens, such as a catalogue, information or booking request app with little or no backend. About KES 50,000 to 150,000 ([Gurucrafts](https://gurucrafts.agency/mobile-app-development-cost-in-kenya/)).

## What drives the cost of an app up or down?

The biggest drivers of the cost to build a mobile app in Kenya are:

1. **Number of screens and user types.** A customer app is one product. Adding a driver app and an admin dashboard roughly triples the work.
2. **The backend.** Accounts, orders, stock and reports live on a server, and that server is often half the project.
3. **Integrations.** M-Pesa, SMS, maps, eTIMS invoices or your existing system each add build and testing time. For instance, M-Pesa integration in an app typically adds KES 20,000 to 80,000 (see our [M-Pesa integration guide](/blog/m-pesa-integration-cost-steps-timeline)).
4. **Design.** Custom illustration and animation cost more than a clean, standard design.
5. **Who builds it.** Kenyan freelance developers charge roughly KES 1,500 to 8,000 an hour ([McTaba](https://mctaba.com/learn/freelancing/freelance-developer-rates-kenya)). Agencies cost more but add design, testing and project management.

## Do you need Android, iOS or both?

Start with Android if your users are in Kenya. In September 2026, **Android ran on 95% of mobile devices in Kenya**, and iOS on under 5% ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/kenya)). With 52.3 million smartphones now connected in Kenya, according to Communications Authority figures for the quarter to June 2026, the Android audience is large ([TechTrends KE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/19/52-million-smartphones-kenya-mobile-data/)).

However, if you sell to corporate clients, tourists or customers abroad, iOS users matter more. That is why we build with **React Native**: one codebase produces both an Android and an iOS app. Cross-platform development typically saves around 30 to 40% compared with building two separate native apps, although the saving shrinks for apps that rely heavily on platform-specific features ([RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/cross-platform-app-development-guide)).

## What does it cost to publish an app on Google Play and the App Store?

Fortunately, the store fees themselves are small:

- **Google Play:** a one-time **US$25** registration fee ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435)).
- **Apple App Store:** **US$99 a year** for the Apple Developer Program ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/)).
- **Store commission:** if you sell digital goods inside the app, both stores take 15% for most small businesses. Physical goods and services, paid through M-Pesa, are not subject to this fee.

One rule often delays launches. New personal Google Play accounts must run a closed test with at least **12 testers for 14 days** before they can publish ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)). Therefore, plan two extra weeks, or register as an organisation.

## What does an app cost after launch?

Budget for these ongoing costs:

- **Maintenance:** about 15 to 20% of the build cost per year, for updates, OS changes and bug fixes. In other words, a KES 300,000 app needs roughly KES 45,000 to 60,000 a year.
- **Hosting and backend:** from a few thousand shillings a month for small apps, rising with users and data.
- **Push notifications:** free through Firebase Cloud Messaging ([Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/pricing)).
- **SMS:** about KES 1 or less per message for one-time codes and alerts, depending on volume.
- **M-Pesa fees:** charged per transaction by Safaricom.

## How long does it take to build an app?

Time also affects the cost to build a mobile app in Kenya. A simple app takes about 4 to 8 weeks. A medium app with accounts and payments usually takes 2 to 4 months. Complex platforms take 5 months or more.

You can shorten that by launching a first version with only the features that prove the idea. Then add the rest based on what real users do.
